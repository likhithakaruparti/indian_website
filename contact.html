Questions everyone asks

Straight answers, including the ones about money.

For a full home, most of our projects land between ₹1,800 and ₹3,200 per square foot depending on materials, joinery and how much civil work is involved. A 1,200 sq ft flat done end-to-end usually sits in the ₹18–30 lakh range. You get a line-by-line BOQ before anything is ordered, and that number is what you pay — we don't revise it midway unless you change the scope yourself.

Design takes three to five weeks. Execution is typically eight to fourteen weeks for an apartment and sixteen to twenty-four for a villa or a commercial fit-out. We give you a dated schedule at sign-off, and 98% of our projects have handed over on or before it. If we are going to slip, you hear it from us early, not on handover day.

Yes — we have delivered in fourteen cities and have permanent studios in Jaipur, Bengaluru and Mumbai. Outside those three, we run the project with a resident site supervisor and our own karigars travel for the joinery and finishing. Travel and lodging for out-of-city projects are billed at cost and shown separately in the BOQ.

The first conversation is free, whether it happens at our studio, at your site or over a video call. We look at your plan, talk through how you live, and give you an honest budget range. There is no charge and no obligation. Design fees only start once you decide to go ahead and we sign a scope.

Absolutely. Plenty of clients have a carpenter they trust or a contractor already on site. In that case we take a design-and-supervision role: you get the full drawing set, material specifications and site visits from us, and your team executes. We are happy to work this way, though the workmanship warranty then sits with your contractor rather than with us.

We do, when it matters to you, and we work with a consultant who has been with the studio for years. Our approach is practical — we adjust entrances, kitchen placement, sleeping directions and light before we ever suggest breaking a wall. If a vastu requirement and a liveability requirement genuinely conflict, we will lay out both options honestly and let you choose.

Not at all. Our Essentials package exists for exactly this, and one of our favourite projects last year was a 180 sq ft pooja room in Pune. A single bedroom, a kitchen or a home office is a perfectly good place to start — and it is a low-risk way to find out whether you like working with us before committing to the whole house.

Design fees are split 50% at sign-off and 50% when the drawing set is delivered. Execution is milestone-based — typically 30% to start, 40% at the joinery stage, 25% before finishing, and the last 5% only after the snag list is closed and you are happy. Everything is invoiced with GST, and we never ask for cash.

Our workmanship is warranted for five years and modular hardware for ten. Call the studio and we send someone — no arguing about whose fault it is first. We also drop in at the three-month mark to check how everything has settled, tighten what needs tightening and fix any small thing you've been meaning to mention.

Yes, and we insist on it. Every project gets a physical sample box — laminates, veneers, fabric swatches, stone chips, paint drawdowns and hardware — brought to your home rather than photographed for a deck. Screens lie about colour, and nobody should approve a ₹20 lakh palette from a JPEG.

Still wondering about something? Call the studio — we like the awkward questions.
