हमारी कहानी

Design that remembers where you come from.

chandrabindu began in a small Jaipur workshop in 2011, with one stubborn idea — that a modern Indian home shouldn't have to choose between comfort and character.

We are a studio of designers, karigars and project managers who believe a jharokha arch belongs just as much in a Gurugram apartment as it does in a haveli. We work with handloom, lime plaster, reclaimed teak and block-printed textiles, and we pair them with the storage, lighting and layouts that a real family needs on a Monday morning.

Every project starts with a long conversation and ends with a home that sounds like you.
